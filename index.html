Where the numbers come from

Every Texas holder of a mixed-beverage permit files a monthly return with the Texas Comptroller of Public Accounts, reporting liquor, wine, beer and cover-charge receipts for each location. The state publishes these returns as open data.

PourSignal loads every return, gives each venue a stable ID, resolves filer and trade names to 93 chains and their parent companies, and computes same-store growth with the 18-month rule restaurant companies use.

Each month is flagged with its filing completeness so a late-filing month is never mistaken for a drop in sales. Recent months can be revised by amended returns.

These are public government records: no consumer data and no material non-public information. Owner names that look like private individuals are withheld. PourSignal is independent and is not affiliated with the State of Texas.
